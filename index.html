Local storage & privacy

Passphrase (optional):

  • Everything runs in your browser. The app has no backend, database, analytics or cloud storage, and sends no project data to any server.
  • Saved drafts are stored in this browser’s local storage. With a passphrase they are encrypted (PBKDF2 + AES-GCM). A forgotten passphrase cannot be recovered.
  • Limits: encryption is only as strong as your passphrase. Without one, the draft is stored as readable text. Malware, browser extensions or someone using your signed-in device can still reach your data. Downloaded .docx, .pdf and .json files are not encrypted. No software is completely immune to attack.
  • Source files and instructions are read and analysed in this browser only (rule-based matching, no AI service). They are not saved with drafts except your typed instructions; use “Remove uploaded files” to clear them. Scanned PDFs and old .doc/.xls files cannot be read locally and are reported, never sent elsewhere.
  • The page loads its open-source libraries (JSZip, docx-preview, html2pdf, pdf.js, SheetJS) as scripts from cdnjs. Your project details are not part of those requests.
